# PRODUCT.md — Durable Product Truth for Roots_Bloom

## 1. Product Identity & Purpose
- **Brand Name:** Roots_Bloom (हस्तनिर्मित मिनिएचर गार्डन्स और लिविंग प्लांटर्स)
- **Tagline:** Living miniature gardens, emotional theme planters & artisan botanical decor.
- **Mission:** Transforming indoor spaces, work desks, and home temples with handcrafted live miniature gardens that tell a story of love, relationship, peace, and living nature.
- **Operating Context:** Direct-to-Consumer (D2C) Indian e-commerce platform with bilingual support (English & Hindi) and pan-India safe transit guarantee.

## 2. Core Target Audience & Emotional Personas
1. **The Thoughtful Gifter:** Looking for meaningful, living gifts for parents, anniversaries, birthdays, housewarmings, or festive celebrations (Maa-Beti, Dada-Dadi, Lovebirds, Couples).
2. **The Nature & Desk Worker:** Urban professionals seeking calming, air-purifying, low-maintenance green companions (ZZ plants, Jade succulents, Sansevieria) for their home office or workstation.
3. **The Devotional & Vastu Believer:** Homes seeking auspicious, serene living greenery (Lord Shiva Meditation, Zen Buddha, Pagoda, Mandir decor).
4. **The Garden & Fairy Terrarium Hobbyist:** Enthusiasts looking for intricate garden miniature figurines, stone bird baths, wooden bridges, street lamps, and DIY accents.

## 3. Brand Voice & Tone
- **Dignified & Handcrafted:** Warm, grounded, sincere, and respectful of nature and relationships.
- **Anti-Hype:** No synthetic marketing buzzwords, no hyperactive pop-ups, no generic dropshipping AI slop.
- **Transparent Guarantees:** 100% Live Plant Transit Replacement Guarantee, Transparent COD & Pricing with inclusive taxes.

## 4. Design Tenets (from Impeccable, Hallmark & Design-MD)
- **Material Grounding:** Warm oat paper and pressed linen surfaces, deep botanical green ink, and warm terracotta accents.
- **Typographic Dignity:** Display Roman serif (`Vollkorn` / `Playfair Display`) carrying emotional storytelling, paired with crystal-clear sans (`Plus Jakarta Sans`) for seamless commercial legibility.
- **Hairlines Over Shadows:** Crisp 1px organic hairline borders (`#E0E7DC`) replace generic, blurry drop-shadows.
- **Silent Craft:** Microinteractions that guide rather than distract. Quick, frictionless 1-click cart and Buy Now flows.
